Assignment 2 · Revenue by city

The goal was to find how much revenue Tulpar Electronics earned in each city in Q3. The Excel analysis removed duplicate orders, subtracted returns, and grouped net revenue by city. Almaty had the highest revenue at 44,954,500 KZT, out of 92,847,000 KZT overall.

Week 11 · Laptop price in Excel

The task was to find the final price of a 319,000 KZT laptop after a 12% discount and 12% VAT. The Excel formula applies the discount first and then adds VAT to the discounted price. The result was 314,406.40 KZT.
